Auth page side images
=====================

Drop two images in THIS folder to replace the plum gradient on the auth pages:

  signin.jpg   -> shown on the Sign in page (left side)
  signup.jpg   -> shown on the Sign up page (left side)

They are served automatically at /signin.jpg and /signup.jpg — no code change needed.
Recommended: tall/portrait images, at least 800x1000px. .jpg or .png (keep the names as above).
Until you add them, a plum gradient shows as a clean fallback.
